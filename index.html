About the School

Sukirti International School is a value-based educational institution committed to intellectual excellence, ethical responsibility, and holistic development.

The school integrates traditional values with modern pedagogy to nurture confident and responsible learners.

Experienced educators ensure personalised attention and balanced growth.

Academic Framework

The curriculum promotes analytical thinking, conceptual clarity, and interactive learning.

Core subjects are supported with digital literacy and environmental education.

Continuous assessment supports academic progress.

Co-Curricular & Other Activities

Sports, yoga, fitness programmes develop discipline and physical well-being.

Music, art, drama, and cultural programmes nurture creativity.

Debates, quizzes, and literary activities enhance communication skills.

Environmental and social initiatives instil civic responsibility.

The school ensures all-round development beyond classrooms.

Principal’s Message

At Sukirti International School, education is a journey of intellectual discovery, moral development, and personal growth. Our mission is to create an environment where every child feels valued, inspired, and empowered to achieve excellence.

We believe that learning should cultivate curiosity, discipline, compassion, and confidence. Through dedicated faculty, structured academics, and co-curricular engagement, we prepare students not only for examinations, but for life.

I invite parents and students to join us in building a strong foundation for a meaningful and successful future.

— Principal, Sukirti International School
